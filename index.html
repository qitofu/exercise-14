Best Asian Snacks

Honey Butter Chips

8.5/10

Honey Butter Chips is a solid 8.5/10 for how crisp each chip is as they linger sweetly on the tongue. The taste isn't too overwhelming, and it pairs nicely with soda. However, eating too many at once makes it not as good.

Want Want Senbei

9/10

Want Want Senbei rice crackers are nostalgic and the first snack I think of when I remember childhood. It is very savory and goes well with tea, and the proportion is just right. A bit too flavorful sometimes, though,

Pocky

7.5/10

While Pocky is a nice, sweet snack to munch on, the coat of chocolate that comes with each flavor can get too sweet and overwhelming. As a result, only a few can be munched on in a sitting.

Turtle Chips

9/10

Turtle Chips come in many different flavors, and all of them have such layers to them that each bite feels satisfying. Not only is it fun to eat, it tastes just as delicious.

Shrimp Chips

8/10

Shrimp Chips have many different brands, but they are all similar in taste. The flavor is light and not too savory, and the crunch is satisfying as well. Once you eat one, you want to eat another.

White Rabbit Candy

9/10

This is another classic nostalgic snack, though it is a candy. Wrapped in rice paper that melts in your mouth, the milky goodness that you taste is addictive and comforting.
